I first came across image steganography during my cybersecurity internship with Edunet Foundation and IBM SkillsBuild. The idea caught my attention: you can hide a message inside an image and later retrieve it.

I built a small tool that let someone encode a message and decode it from the same image. I’ve carried that idea into the Secret Layer of this portfolio as a way to make exploring it a little more hands-on. I hope you enjoy it, have a good one !

I like projects like this because they make you curious. You have to look closer, try things, and work out what’s happening. It reflects the kind of problem-solving I enjoy: experimenting, learning as I go, and making technical ideas more engaging. It also allowed me to delve into cybersecurity, the basics including threats, vulnerabilities , industry best-practices and understand the pace at which technology has evolved, from the steam engines to the first industrial revolution ,from microprocessors to Artificial Intelligence.
