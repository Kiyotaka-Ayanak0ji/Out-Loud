Out Loud / Design Lab

A · Frequency B · Decoder C · Glow D · Paper & Ink

Four directions. One skeleton.

Each option is a working miniature of the full Out Loud page — top bar with the animation toggle, hero with transcript player, chapter links, an expandable decision card, the voice-profile demo, the clue puzzle, receipts, projects, about and contact. All content is placeholder. Every option keeps your palette: ink #0F172A, indigo, teal, coral, amber.

Option A

Frequency — kinetic editorial

Heavyweight Archivo display type with a masked line-by-line hero reveal, a live waveform motif, colored chapter rails and one slow editorial marquee. Confident, loud, built for the "built by voice" story.

Option B

Decoder — terminal craft

Space Grotesk headings with monospace chrome, a blueprint grid backdrop, sharp cards with corner ticks and hard offset buttons. Feels like cracking a code — the Secret Layer's native language.

Option C

Glow — soft premium glass

Sora headings, rounded glass cards with ambient accent glows behind each chapter, floating pill buttons. Calm, modern and product-like — the friendliest of the four.

Option D

Paper & Ink — print soul

Fraunces serif display, warm paper panels set against the ink base, perforated thermal-receipt cards for Voice Receipts, and hard offset-shadow buttons. Warm, tactile and the most unexpected.
