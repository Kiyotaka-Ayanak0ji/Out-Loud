In 2024, I helped manage an event for my college’s E-Cell initiative. Our team explored an idea for a voice-based service that could support daily-wage workers, especially when language or access makes everyday tasks harder.

The idea was to make useful information easier to reach through the languages people already speak. We also discussed helping workers find opportunities and understand things like fair pay. It was an early concept, but it made me think about who gets left out when technology assumes everyone is comfortable with English or a keyboard. I also believe this is an untapped market that if utilized properly could help instill trust, provide growth opportunities and benefit the regional communities.

I’ve included a small demonstration in this portfolio to show the idea. The sample information is only illustrative; it isn’t real worker data. For me, this project is a reminder that technology matters most when it makes something genuinely easier for someone.
